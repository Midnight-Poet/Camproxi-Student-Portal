# Camproxi - Core Features Documentation

Camproxi is a comprehensive student portal designed to connect university students with everything they need around their campus, in one place. 

## 1. Lodge Discovery and Housing
* **Browse Lodges**: View detailed listings of available off-campus lodges, apartments, and hostels around the university.
* **Advanced Filtering**: Filter housing options by price range, distance from campus, number of rooms, and availability.
* **Interactive Map View**: A dynamic map interface centered on the selected campus, showing lodge locations with custom markers and cover images for easy spatial context.
* **Show Interest**: Easily express interest in a lodge to contact landlords or property managers directly.

## 2. Campus Marketplace
* **Food Orders**: Browse local vendors and restaurants, place food orders for delivery or pickup.
* **Groceries**: Shop for daily essentials and groceries from vendors around the campus.
* **Services**: Book everyday services such as laundry, cleaning, and technical repairs, tailored to the student community.

## 3. Seamless Onboarding and Authentication
* **Student Verification**: Secure registration system with email and phone verification to ensure a trusted community of students.
* **Campus Selection**: Students are tied to a specific campus region, ensuring that all listings, services, and maps are hyper-local and relevant to them.

## 4. Personalization and Settings
* **Profile Management**: Maintain a personal student profile with a verified status badge.
* **Custom Preferences**: Adjust local preferences such as currency, distance units (km/miles), and language.
* **Notification Controls**: Granular control over notifications for new listings, price drops, messages, and promotions.
* **Privacy & Security**: Tools to manage location services, activity status visibility, and password management.

## 5. Saved Items and Activity Tracking
* **Saved Listings**: Bookmark favorite lodges, vendors, or services for quick access later.
* **Activity History**: Keep track of recent searches, viewed listings, and requests.
